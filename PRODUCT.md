# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Active Korean retail stock investors, colloquially known as **개미** (Ants), who often fall victim to emotional trading, panic selling, or buying at the top of market hype (FOMO). They seek objective, data-driven contrarian entries but lack the tools or time to cross-reference chaotic internet community sentiment (such as Naver stock discussion boards) with actual mathematical technical indicators.

## Product Purpose

개미반대로 (Ant-Contra) is an interactive, real-time contrarian stock scanner and dashboard. It filters the noise of retail trading communities to isolate extreme fear and panic ("비명 지수" or Scream Index) and cross-references it with classic momentum/volume indicators (RSI, Bollinger Bands, Volume, Foreign Buying) to identify high-probability reversal setups. Success is defined as providing retail investors with clear, calm, and highly visual contrarian buying signals, converting overwhelming sentiment noise into actionable trade ideas.

## Brand Personality

-The brand is **friendly, approachable, and highly visual**. Unlike complex, clinical trading terminals, it speaks to everyday retail investors with a warm, encouraging, yet quantitative voice. It acts as a supportive companion that helps investors stay objective when the crowd is panicking.
+The brand is **calm, precise, and highly visual** — a minimal fintech aesthetic (clean white cards on a soft blue-gray canvas, single-typeface Pretendard hierarchy) closer to Korean fintech apps (e.g. Toss) than a warm illustrated companion. Unlike complex, clinical trading terminals, it speaks to everyday retail investors with a reassuring, quantitative voice rather than playful decoration. It acts as a steady, objective counterpart that keeps investors grounded when the crowd is panicking.

## Anti-references

This product must explicitly avoid looking like a cold, dry, generic corporate SaaS dashboard or an overwhelming trading terminal crowded with thousands of flashing tables. It also rejects the look of amateurish, spam-filled stock-alert websites.

## Design Principles

### Clear Signal Over Noise

Prioritize clear visual hierarchies and synthesized indicators (like gauges and status tiers) so that investors can assess market sentiment and indicators at a single glance without scrolling through complex raw logs.

### Approachable Objectivity

Explain technical metrics in simple, conversational Korean rather than dense financial jargon. Combine professional data visualization (e.g., Plotly charts) with friendly and clear layout cards to make quant-style investing feel accessible to beginners.

### Calming Guardrails

Use structured layouts, consistent padding, and cohesive background tones to project stability. Visual cues should act as psychological guardrails, keeping the user grounded, rational, and immune to market FOMO or panic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Adhere to standard web accessibility guidelines, with a focus on high color contrast for text readability. Provide clear typographic scaling to accommodate diverse screen sizes and devices. Ensure stock colors (such as the East Asian custom of using red for upward movements and blue/green for downward movements) are accompanied by explicit text labels, shapes, or secondary cues to support color-blind users.
